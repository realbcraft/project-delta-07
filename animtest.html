Scroll-driven animation test

Four ways to make the page play like an animation while you scroll. Scroll slowly and watch, then scroll back up: each one rewinds. Under each title there is an idea for where it could live on the homepage.

This browser doesn’t support scroll-driven animations yet, so you’re seeing the finished state of each demo. Try a recent Chrome, Edge or Safari.

A thumbnail builds itself

The page pins in place and your scroll plays a sketch turning into a finished thumbnail: blueprint lines, colour, character, title, glow. It shows off your craft in a way a static image can’t.

Could go: right after the hero, as a “how a design comes together” moment before Featured Work. Swap my placeholder art for one of your real pieces, split into layers.

Scroll down, gallery moves sideways
